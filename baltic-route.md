## 🇵🇱 Poland: Gdynia, Gdańsk & Malbork

Explore the premier sights, unique seasonal experiences, artisan treats, and authentic dining across 🇵🇱 Poland: Gdynia, Gdańsk & Malbork.

### 🏛️ Top Attractions & Sights

| Attraction / Landmark | Type & Focus | Highlights & What to Expect | Practical Visitor Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Museum of the Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museum_of_the_Second_World_War) (Muzeum II Wojny Światowej)** | Cultural Landmark & Museum | 14-meter underground permanent exhibition exploring civilian wartime life, dramatic immersive street sets, tanks, and multimedia archives (exceptional audio guides tailored for teenagers). | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
| **[Długi Targ & Neptune's Fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_Market)** | Iconic Sight & Scenic Landmark | Gdańsk’s iconic royal thoroughfare flanked by pastel Dutch-mannerist burgher facades, the historic Main Town Hall, and bronze Fountain of Neptune illuminated by Christmas lights. | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
| **[Gdańsk Medieval Port Crane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gda%C5%84sk) (Żuraw)** | Iconic Sight & Scenic Landmark | Branch of the National Maritime Museum along the Motława riverfront; 15th-century fortified twin-tower brick gate housing the world’s largest wooden port treadwheel crane. | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
| **[Malbork Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malbork_Castle) (Zamek w Malborku)** | Historic Castle & Fortress | UNESCO World Heritage site and the largest brick castle complex on earth, founded in the 13th century by the Teutonic Knights; features the Grand Master’s Palace, medieval heating ducts, Knight's armory, and moat battlements (high-tech audio guide automatically triggers based on GPS location as you explore). | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |

## 🇱🇹 Lithuania: Vilnius & Trakai

Explore the premier sights, unique seasonal experiences, artisan treats, and authentic dining across 🇱🇹 Lithuania: Vilnius & Trakai.

### 🏛️ Top Attractions & Sights

| Attraction / Landmark | Type & Focus | Highlights & What to Expect | Practical Visitor Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Vilnius Old Town & Cathedral Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Town_of_Vilnius)** | Sacred Architecture & Heritage | One of Northern Europe’s largest surviving medieval UNESCO Baroque old towns; neoclassical Vilnius Cathedral, freestanding 57m belfry, and Gediminas’ Tower crowning the snowy hill. | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
| **[Republic of Užupis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U%C5%BEupis)** | Iconic Sight & Scenic Landmark | Bohemian self-declared artistic republic across the Vilnia river with its own president, quirky 41-clause human/animal rights constitution mounted on polished bronze mirrors, and whimsically painted art ateliers. | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
| **[Trakai Island Castle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trakai_Island_Castle) (Trakų salos pilis)** | Historic Castle & Fortress | Fairy-tale 14th-century red-brick fortress perched on an island in Lake Galvė, accessed via long wooden footbridges over frozen waters; houses the Trakai History Museum and medieval weapon vaults. | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
| **[Hill of Crosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hill_of_Crosses) (Kryžių kalnas)** | Sacred Architecture & Heritage | World-famous pilgrimage knoll near Šiauliai covered with over 100,000 wooden and metal crosses, rosaries, and carvings (visited en route to Riga; completely outdoors and accessible 24/7 on Christmas Eve). | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
