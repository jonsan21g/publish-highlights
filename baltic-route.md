## 🇱🇹 Lithuania: Vilnius & Trakai

Explore the premier sights, unique seasonal experiences, artisan treats, and authentic dining across 🇱🇹 Lithuania: Vilnius & Trakai.

### 🏛️ Top Attractions & Sights

| **[Vilnius Old Town & Cathedral Square](https** | Sacred Architecture & Heritage | //en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Town_of_Vilnius): One of Northern Europe’s largest surviving medieval UNESCO Baroque old towns; neoclassical Vilnius Cathedral, freestanding 57m belfry, and Gediminas’ Tower crowning the snowy hill. | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |

| **[Hill of Crosses](https** | Sacred Architecture & Heritage | //en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hill_of_Crosses) (Kryžių kalnas): World-famous pilgrimage knoll near Šiauliai covered with over 100,000 wooden and metal crosses, rosaries, and carvings (visited en route to Riga; completely outdoors and accessible 24/7 on Christmas Eve). | Plan visits early or at golden hour for the best photography light and relaxed exploration. |
