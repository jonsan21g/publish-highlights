## 🇨🇿 Prague, Czech Republic

Prague is one of Europe's best-preserved medieval and Baroque capitals, featuring cobblestone alleys, illuminated river vistas, and a rich cafe and brewery culture.

### 🏛️ Top Attractions & Sights

| Attraction / Landmark | Type & Focus | Highlights & What to Expect | Practical Visitor Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Klementinum Baroque Library](https://www.klementinum.com/en/)** | Baroque Architecture & Astronomy | World-renowned Baroque Library Hall with gilded globes and ceiling frescoes by Jan Hiebl; Meridian Hall; 68m Astronomical Tower with 360° city panorama. | Book guided tour slots online in advance; dusk visits offer magnificent city illuminations. |
| **[Old Town Square & Astronomical Clock](https://prague.eu/en/object/places/312/prague-astronomical-clock-staromestsky-orloj)** | Medieval Historic Center | Historic square anchored by the 600-year-old Astronomical Clock (*Pražský orloj*) with mechanical hourly chime, Gothic Týn Church, and seasonal festive markets. | Arrive 10 minutes before the top of the hour to catch the Apostles' procession; free public access. |
| **[Prague Castle Complex (*Pražský hrad*)](https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors)** | Royal Citadel & Cathedral | Monumental royal fortress covering **[St. Vitus Cathedral](https://www.katedralasvatehovita.cz/en)** (stained glass), Old Royal Palace (Vladislav Hall), St. George's Basilica, and fairy-tale **[Golden Lane (*Zlatá ulička*)](https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors/objects-for-visitors/golden-lane-10332)** with miniature 16th-century cottages. | Circuit ticket covers all key sites; morning visits offer the lightest crowds and best photography. |
| **[Charles Bridge (*Karlův most*)](https://www.prague.eu/en/object/places/93/charles-bridge-karluv-most)** | 14th-Century Gothic Bridge | Iconic stone pedestrian bridge lined with 30 Baroque statues spanning the Vltava river, framed by Old Town and Lesser Town bridge towers. | Visit at early morning sunrise or twilight when the 19th-century gas streetlamps are lit. |
| **[Petřín Lookout Tower](https://muzeumprahy.cz/en/petrin-lookout-tower)** | Panoramic Observation Deck | 63.5m miniature replica of the Eiffel Tower set high on Petřín Hill, offering panoramic views over Prague's hundred spires. | Accessible via a scenic ridge stroll from Strahov Monastery or via the Petřín Funicular. |
| **[John Lennon Wall & Kampa Island](https://www.prague.eu/en/object/places/1208/lennon-wall)** | Urban Art & Riverside Charm | Constantly evolving graffiti wall of peace messages and lyrics; adjacent to romantic historic watermills on Devil’s Stream (*Čertovka*). | Free 24/7 public access; easily combined with a riverside stroll on Kampa Island. |
| **[Wenceslas Square](https://www.trhypraha.cz/en/)** | Historic Boulevard & Markets | Vast central boulevard flanked by historic buildings, shops, and festive chalets selling handmade wooden crafts, ornaments, and hot spiced drinks. | Flat, pedestrian-friendly walking avenue connecting New Town to Old Town. |

## 🇦🇹 Vienna, Austria

Vienna blends imperial grandeur, grand coffeehouse traditions, and world-class classical arts with vibrant seasonal open-air celebrations.

### 🏛️ Top Attractions & Sights

| Attraction / Landmark | Type & Focus | Highlights & What to Expect | Practical Visitor Tips |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Schönbrunn Palace Grand Tour](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/)** | Imperial Habsburg Palace | 40 lavish state rooms of Emperor Franz Joseph and Empress Sisi, including the Great Gallery and Hall of Ceremonies; framed by vast baroque palace gardens. | Pre-book timed-entry tickets online to avoid ticket hall queues; reachable via U-Bahn U4 (Schönbrunn). |
| **[St. Stephen's Cathedral (*Stephansdom*)](https://www.stephanskirche.at/)** | Gothic Masterpiece | Vienna's spiritual landmark; soaring 136m South Tower, intricate stone pulpit by Anton Pilgram, and colorful glazed diamond-patterned roof tiles. | Central location at Stephansplatz (U1/U3); free access to rear nave, modest fee for tower or catacomb tours. |
| **[Imperial Hofburg Palace Complex](https://www.hofburg-wien.at/en/)** | Imperial Residence & Courts | St. Michael’s Wing, inner courtyards (*In der Burg*), vast Heldenplatz, and Renaissance courtyard of the **[Spanish Riding School Stallburg](https://www.srs.at/en/)** to view the historic white Lipizzaner stallions. | Extensive pedestrian palace grounds; free to stroll courtyards 24/7. |
| **[Upper Belvedere Palace](https://belvedere.at/en)** | High Baroque Art Museum | Baroque palace complex set on a panoramic hill overlooking Vienna; home to Gustav Klimt's world-famous gold-leaf masterpieces ***The Kiss*** and *Judith*, plus works by Egon Schiele. | Pre-book timed admission tickets online; early morning slots offer the best viewing experience. |
| **[Karlskirche (St. Charles Church)](https://www.karlskirche.at/)** | High Baroque Cathedral | Monumental domed church flanked by colossal twin relief columns modeled after Trajan’s Column, with a large reflective pond glowing at dusk. | Located at Karlsplatz; beautiful illuminated photo vantage point from the plaza gardens. |
| **[Hundertwasserhaus](https://www.hundertwasser-haus.info/en/)** | Expressionist Architecture | Whimsical, undulating apartment landmark designed by Friedensreich Hundertwasser; features patchwork facade colors, wavy floors, onion turrets, and over 200 trees growing on balconies. | Exterior viewing on Kegelgasse; visit adjacent Hundertwasser Village for souvenirs and cafes. |
| **[The Imperial Promenade (Graben & Kohlmarkt)](https://www.wien.info/en/shopping-wining-dining/shopping/luxury-shopping-354960)** | Elegant Pedestrian Avenues | Grand pedestrian boulevards lined with historic palaces, court confectionery shops, luxury boutiques, and the Baroque **[Plague Column (*Pestsäule*)](https://www.wien.info/en/sightseeing/sights/plague-column-341854)**. | Illuminated by oversized chandelier light installations in winter. |
| **[Rathausplatz & City Hall Plaza](https://www.christkindlmarkt.at/)** | Neo-Gothic Landmark | Neo-Gothic City Hall plaza hosting grand seasonal markets, festive illuminations, and the glowing Tree of Hearts. | Free public access; served directly by tram lines 1, 2, and D. |
