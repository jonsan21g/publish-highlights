Central Europe Winter Highlights

🇨🇿 Czech Republic • 🇦🇹 Austria • 🇸🇰 Slovakia • 🇩🇪 Germany

A comprehensive guide spanning Prague's medieval towers, Český Krumlov, Vienna's imperial palaces and grand coffeehouses, Bratislava's clifftop castle, Ramsau alpine sleigh rides, Salzburg's fortress, the 14 km Wildkogel toboggan run, fairytale Schloss Neuschwanstein, and Munich's hypercars and beer halls.

  • 🏛️ Sights: Klementinum, Schönbrunn Palace, Hohensalzburg, Neuschwanstein.
  • 🎯 Activities: Lipno bobsleigh, Wildkogel 14 km sled run, Red Bull Hangar-7, Allianz Arena.
  • ☕ Street Treats: Trdelník, Cornetto Cappuccino, Bosna, Germknödel, Schmalznudel.
  • 🍽️ Dining: Historic breweries, tavern roasts, plus popular Asian dining alternatives.
